## What's new in Scruffy 0.6.1

- **Counts read correctly.** A count of one now reads "1 file", "1 video" and "1 of 1 file processed" instead of "1 files" and the like. This covers the status line, the job list, the review sheet, recovery history, the video pane and the purgeable-space notes.

Everything else is as in 0.6.0.

## Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.

## Install

Open `Scruffy.dmg` and drag Scruffy to Applications.
