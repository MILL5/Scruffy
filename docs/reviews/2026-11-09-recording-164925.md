## What works well

- Active category highlighting is clear and persists across navigation.
- The settled views have correct category totals and descending file sizes.
- Search is present in each shown section, and the select-all scope is explained by its nearby count.
- Browser caches explicitly excludes passwords, cookies, bookmarks and history. Cleanup and compression retain separate review actions, with nothing selected automatically in the sampled screens.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Fix atomic category transitions and add a rapid-navigation regression covering header, rows, count and pager together.
2. Protect incomplete-download artifacts, including revalidation when a selected file becomes a pending download before its move.
3. Replace the one-category donut with a compact summary, then use the recovered space for readable decision metadata and clearly scoped counts.

The clip covers navigation among populated sections. Those need separate interaction tests; no worker-progress defect is inferred from the absence of an active job panel here.
